Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

KingPi is an independent community-project directory prototype. It is not an official Pi Network product and is not affiliated with the Pi Core Team.

Information and current functionality

The current public prototype is intended for browsing sample project information. It does not provide Pi authentication, payment processing, user accounts, or donation collection. Do not submit sensitive personal information through the site.

Wallet credentials

KingPi does not ask for, collect, or store wallet passphrases, seed phrases, private keys, or recovery phrases. Never share these credentials with KingPi or anyone else.

Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted using Vercel. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs to deliver and secure the website, subject to its own privacy terms.

Third-party services

Links to third-party websites or services are governed by their own privacy policies. KingPi is not responsible for their practices.

Children

The prototype is not designed to collect information from children.

Changes

If KingPi adds accounts, analytics, authentication, or payment features, this policy will be reviewed and updated before those features are used.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the KingPi site administrator through the official project communication channel.
